Hydrogen Peroxide Sensitivity Connects the Activity of COX5A and NPR3 to the Regulation of YAP1 Expression

Authors: Sarah Takallou, Maryam Hajikarimlou, Mustafa Al-Gafari, Jiashu Wang, Sasi Kumar Jagadeesan, Thomas David Daniel Kazmirchuk, Houman Moteshareie, Alex Mulet Indrayanti, Taha Azad, Martin Holcik, Bahram Samanfar, Myron Smith, Ashkan Golshani

Publication type: Original research
Open access: Unconfirmed (FASEB Journal is generally open access)

Study Overview

This study examined regulation of YAP1 expression under oxidative stress, focusing on the roles of COX5A and NPR3 in the yeast hydrogen peroxide stress response.

Key Findings

  • YAP1 mRNA undergoes IRES-mediated translation in the presence of hydrogen peroxide (H₂O₂).
  • COX5A and NPR3 facilitate YAP1 translation via the YAP1 IRES, directly or indirectly.

Why This Study Matters

Adds to understanding of alternative translation-initiation mechanisms activated during oxidative stress in yeast.

Scientific Questions This Paper Informs

  • How do COX5A and NPR3 influence IRES-mediated translation of YAP1 under oxidative stress?
  • What broader role does IRES-mediated translation play in yeast stress responses?
